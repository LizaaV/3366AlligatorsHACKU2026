I've built **Earth Agent.dc.html** as one interactive prototype using the dark design from your design.md. I checked the first screen, the dry-patch question run, the presets panel and a preset's full view in my own preview. A second check of the whole thing is still running.

- **Start screen:** a 3D Earth on the right that spins and can be dragged. The chat box sits at the top left with three example questions, and a short headline sits below it.
- **AI thinking steps:** ask "Where are the dry patches in my field?" and the answer is built step by step, following your 10 steps ("Accessed Sentinel-2", "Preparing a contour mask", and so on). The first step stops to ask about crop, watering and when the problem started. The view then zooms into the field and adds the AI layers. The answer shows the dry area, the likely cause and what to do, with **Save as artifact** and **Add trigger** buttons.
- **Toolbar:** a Google Earth–style bar of my own design. It has place search, undo/redo, go to coordinates, placemark, draw (polygon, circle or upload an outline), your saved field outlines, measure and reference image.
- **Layers and timeline:** six layers you can switch on and off: field outline, plant water (NDMI), plant health (NDVI), temperature, dry spots and clouds. The timeline has 8 satellite dates with a play button and a slider, and the dry patch grows as you move through the dates.
- **Explore:** a panel slides up from the bottom. The 9 categories are a Minecraft-style block bar you can switch with keys 1–9 or the scroll wheel. Each category has a sliding row of 3 presets. Clicking a preset opens a full view with satellite, cost, developer, reference image, description, and "Try it on my contour".
- **My dashboard:** two dashboards made of artifact cards and graphs, which you can customise (remove cards, add a new dashboard). You can also ask the dashboard questions about its data.
- **Triggers:** alerts with on/off switches. You can also create one straight from an AI answer.

Things to know:
- The map images are free Sentinel-2 cloud-free pictures from 2020, not live data.
- All AI answers, numbers and the dashboard replies are scripted for the mockup.
- Only the dry-patch question has its own full set of steps. Other questions and presets reuse a general version of the same steps.
- The layout is designed for screens about 1280px wide or more. At smaller widths the toolbar drops undo/redo to fit.
